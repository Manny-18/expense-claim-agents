# Meridian Consulting India Pvt. Ltd.
## Travel and Expense (T&E) Reimbursement Policy, Version 3.2
Effective 01 April 2026. Supersedes Version 3.1. Owner: Finance Shared Services, Gurugram.

---

### TE-01. Scope and Principles

TE-01.1 This policy applies to all full time employees, retainers and interns of Meridian Consulting India Pvt. Ltd. who incur business expenses on behalf of the company or a client.

TE-01.2 Three principles govern every claim: the expense must be **business necessary**, **reasonable in amount**, and **adequately evidenced**. A claim that fails any one of the three is not reimbursable even if the amount falls within a limit.

TE-01.3 Reimbursement is a settlement of costs already borne by the employee. It is not an allowance, and an unspent entitlement cannot be claimed.

TE-01.4 Where a client contract specifies a stricter expense term, the client term prevails and the difference is borne by the practice cost centre, not by the employee.

---

### TE-02. Grade Bands and Entitlement Matrix

TE-02.1 Entitlements are set by grade band. The five bands are: **B1 Analyst**, **B2 Associate**, **B3 Consultant**, **B4 Manager**, **B5 Partner and above**.

TE-02.2 Daily hotel room rent ceilings, exclusive of taxes, are:

| Grade | Metro (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune) | Non Metro |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | INR 4,000 | INR 2,800 |
| B2 | INR 5,500 | INR 3,800 |
| B3 | INR 7,500 | INR 5,000 |
| B4 | INR 10,000 | INR 7,000 |
| B5 | INR 15,000 | INR 11,000 |

TE-02.3 Daily meal ceilings, inclusive of taxes, where the employee is on an overnight outstation trip:

| Grade | Metro | Non Metro |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | INR 1,200 | INR 900 |
| B2 | INR 1,500 | INR 1,100 |
| B3 | INR 2,000 | INR 1,400 |
| B4 | INR 2,500 | INR 1,800 |

TE-02.4 Air travel class entitlement: B1 to B3 travel economy on domestic and economy on international sectors under six hours. B4 travels economy domestic and premium economy on international sectors over six hours. B5 may travel business class on any sector over three hours.

TE-02.5 Rail travel entitlement: B1 and B2 travel AC 3 Tier or AC Chair Car. B3 and above travel AC 2 Tier. Rajdhani, Shatabdi and Vande Bharat services are permitted for all grades in the entitled class.

### TE-03. Meals and Entertainment

TE-03.1 Meal ceilings in TE-02.3 are **daily caps, not per bill caps**. Where multiple meal bills fall on the same calendar date, the sum is tested against the ceiling.

TE-03.2 Alcohol is **not reimbursable** in any circumstance, including where it appears as a line item on an otherwise valid restaurant bill. The alcohol value plus proportionate tax must be deducted before the claim is submitted.

TE-03.3 Client entertainment is reimbursable only where (a) a client attendee is named on the claim, (b) the engagement code is stated, and (c) pre approval from a B4 or above exists where the bill exceeds INR 8,000. Unnamed attendees make the claim non reimbursable.

TE-03.4 Team meals internal to Meridian are capped at INR 800 per attendee and require the attendee list. They are permitted a maximum of twice per calendar month per team.

TE-03.5 Meals on a same day return trip with no overnight stay are capped at 50 percent of the applicable daily meal ceiling.

### TE-04. Local Conveyance and Mileage

TE-04.1 Point to point cab fares in the city of posting are reimbursable only where the journey is business purposed and starts or ends at a client site, an airport, a railway station or the Meridian office outside the window 09:00 to 19:00.

TE-04.2 Home to office and office to home travel is **not reimbursable** between 09:00 and 19:00 on a working day. It is reimbursable outside that window where the employee has worked late or travelled early at client request.

TE-04.3 Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **INR 12.00 per kilometre for a four wheeler** and **INR 4.50 per kilometre for a two wheeler**, inclusive of fuel, toll and maintenance. A separate fuel bill cannot be claimed in addition to mileage for the same journey.

TE-04.4 Single cab journeys exceeding INR 2,500 require a written justification on the claim. Journeys exceeding INR 5,000 require pre approval from a B4 or above.

TE-04.5 Airport transfers are reimbursable at actuals in the entitled category. Luxury or premium cab categories are reimbursable only for B4 and above.

### TE-05. Documentary Evidence

TE-05.1 A **GST compliant tax invoice** is required for every claim line of INR 1,000 or more. The invoice must carry the vendor GSTIN, the invoice number, the invoice date and the taxable value.

TE-05.2 For claim lines below INR 1,000, a payment receipt, a card charge slip or a UPI transaction reference is acceptable.

TE-05.3 Hotel invoices must be in the name of the employee or of Meridian Consulting India Pvt. Ltd. A third party name invalidates the claim.

TE-05.4 Where a receipt is genuinely lost, a **Missing Receipt Declaration** countersigned by the reporting manager is permitted for claim lines up to INR 2,000, limited to **two instances per employee per financial quarter**. The third such instance in a quarter is rejected.

TE-05.5 Scanned or photographed receipts are acceptable. The image must be legible: the vendor name, date and total must be readable.

### TE-06. Goods and Services Tax

TE-06.1 Meridian claims input tax credit on eligible expenses. A vendor GSTIN must be a valid 15 character identifier: two digit state code, ten character PAN, one entity code, the letter Z in position fourteen, and a checksum character.

TE-06.2 Input tax credit is **blocked under Section 17(5) of the CGST Act** on food and beverage, outdoor catering, club membership and health or fitness centre expenses. Such lines are reimbursed to the employee at the gross invoice value and no credit is taken.

TE-06.3 Hotel accommodation outside the state of registration generates CGST and SGST of the supplier state, on which credit is not available to Meridian. The claim is still reimbursed at gross.

TE-06.4 Air and rail travel invoices must carry the Meridian GSTIN **06AAJCM4321L1ZE** where the booking was made through the corporate travel desk. A missing corporate GSTIN on a travel desk booking is a recoverable error, not a rejection: the claim is approved and the invoice is sent back to the travel desk for amendment.

### TE-07. Submission Timelines

TE-07.1 A claim must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense date.

TE-07.2 Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days from the expense date require a written justification and approval from a B4 or above. They are not automatically rejected.

TE-07.3 Claims submitted **more than 60 days** after the expense date are **rejected** and are not payable, irrespective of merit.

TE-07.4 All claims for a financial year must be submitted by 15 April of the following financial year regardless of the 30 day rule.

---

### TE-08. Approval Authority

TE-08.1 Approval thresholds on the total claim value:

| Total claim value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to INR 10,000 | Reporting manager |
| INR 10,001 to INR 50,000 | Practice head (B4 or above) |
| INR 50,001 to INR 2,00,000 | Finance controller |
| Above INR 2,00,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

TE-08.2 Self approval is prohibited. A claim cannot be approved by the claimant even where the claimant holds the required authority.

TE-08.3 An expense already billed to and recovered from a client must be marked **client rechargeable** with the engagement code. Failure to mark a rechargeable expense is a control breach reported to the engagement partner.

---

### TE-09. Duplicate and Integrity Controls

TE-09.1 A claim line is a **suspected duplicate** where the same vendor, the same date and an amount within one percent of a previously submitted line appear in any claim by the same employee.

TE-09.2 A claim line is a **suspected split** where two or more lines from the same vendor on the same date each fall just below an approval or evidence threshold but together exceed it.

TE-09.3 Round amount patterns, meaning three or more lines in a single claim ending in exactly 00 above INR 1,000, are a flag for review, not an automatic rejection.

TE-09.4 Expenses on dates when the employee was on approved leave, or on a public holiday with no client engagement recorded, require explanation.

TE-09.5 A claim found to be deliberately falsified is referred to Human Resources under the Code of Conduct. The AI review system **never makes this determination**; it may only escalate with reasons.

---

### TE-10. Non Reimbursable Items

TE-10.1 The following are never reimbursable: alcohol, tobacco, personal grooming, in room entertainment and minibar, traffic fines and penalties, passport and personal visa fees other than business visas, personal insurance, loyalty programme fees, personal shopping, gym and club membership, baggage beyond the airline free allowance except where business material is carried, and spouse or family travel.

TE-10.2 Laundry is reimbursable only on outstation trips of four consecutive nights or more, capped at INR 500 per trip.

TE-10.3 Tips and service charges are reimbursable up to 10 percent of the bill value where not already included.

---

### TE-11. Decision Outcomes

TE-11.1 Every reviewed claim carries one of four outcomes: **APPROVE**, **PARTIAL APPROVE** where specific lines are disallowed and the remainder is paid, **REJECT**, or **ESCALATE** where human judgement is required.

TE-11.2 A disallowance must cite the policy clause that supports it. An uncited disallowance is invalid and must be treated as an escalation.

TE-11.3 Where the evidence is ambiguous, the reviewer escalates rather than rejects. The burden of a clear rejection rests on the reviewer, not on the employee.

TE-11.4 Any claim with a suspected integrity breach under TE-09 is escalated to the Finance Controller and never auto rejected.
